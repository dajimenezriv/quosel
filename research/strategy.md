## Phase 0 — Discovery & strategy (before touching any tool)

Understand the business: which services actually make money, the exact service area, who the customer is, what the goal is. Lock the canonical NAP and brand name. Then do keyword research + intent mapping, and competitor/SERP analysis — who ranks, in pack vs organic, how strong, how many reviews, what their pages look like. This tells you difficulty and what "good" looks like. This phase is exactly where your best skill — instinct for what's right for the business — applies. Point it here.

## Phase 1 — Foundation & assets

1. GBP: claim, verify, and fully optimize — correct primary + secondary categories, services, description, photos, hours, service area.
2. Website: homepage + service pages + location pages (as the research dictates), on-page SEO targeting your mapped keywords, LocalBusiness schema, NAP in the footer, fast and mobile — where your static Quosel architecture is a real edge.
3. Tracking: set up GSC (it attaches to the site, so it comes here, not first), GA4, and call/form tracking so you can measure leads, not just rank. Submit the sitemap.
4. Baseline: capture starting positions (your first Local Falcon scan is a baseline, not a verdict), plus GSC and GBP baselines.

## Phase 2 — Authority / prominence (the ongoing grind you were missing). This is where rankings are earned over weeks and months

1. Citations: consistent NAP across the key Spanish directories.
2. Reviews: a system to generate them steadily and respond to every one.
3. Content: keep expanding to cover more of the keyword map; GBP posts.
4. Links: local backlinks — associations, suppliers, local press, sponsorships.

## Phase 3 — Measure, iterate, repeat

Local Falcon grid scans for map-pack coverage, GSC for organic queries/impressions/positions, conversion tracking for the actual leads. Read the weak areas, feed them back into Phase 2, re-measure on a monthly-ish cadence. This never really ends — it's the loop, not a finish line.
